# Aerofit-Descriptive-Statistics-Probability
Aerofit Business Case Study – Descriptive Statistics, Probability Analysis, EDA, and Customer Profiling.

## Business Problem
Analyze customer characteristics across Aerofit's three treadmill products (KP281, KP481, and KP781) and identify patterns that can help with customer profiling and product recommendations.

## Analysis
- Exploratory Data Analysis
- Descriptive Statistics
- Outlier Analysis
- Probability & Conditional Probability
- Bayes' Theorem
- Correlation Analysis
- Customer Profiling

## Key Insights
- KP281 accounts for 44.4% of purchases.
- KP781 customers generally have higher income, fitness levels, usage, and planned mileage.
- Income and planned mileage show stronger relationships with product choice than age.
- KP281 and KP481 have significant overlap in customer characteristics.
